GlobalDiscount does not process online payments or collect payment card or bank information through the application.

GlobalDiscount may request access to your device's location to provide location-based features, such as finding nearby vendors.

Location information may be used while the relevant feature is being used. GlobalDiscount does not continuously track your location.

You can control location permissions through your device settings. Disabling location access may prevent some location-based features from working.
